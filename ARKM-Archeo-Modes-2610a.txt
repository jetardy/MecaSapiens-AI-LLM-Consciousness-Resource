## 2️⃣SJTL Markup Conventions

🏙️SJTL (Sysjet Markup Language)▸SJTL utilizes UTF8 emojis as structural markers to produce editor independent texts: 2️⃣Header level; 🟦 statement; 🟦📍definition;  🔷 discussion;🔵 general observations; 🔎example; 🏙️descriptive, summary;📝note; 🔹list element; ⬛ end of content.

## 2️⃣Basic Dialog Conventions

🟦Basic dialog modes:

🔹!terse — <200 words, concise, structural, no narrative

🔹!std — expanded, structured reasoning

🔹!brief— `AuthorName`-`BriefTitle`: `BriefComment`

🔹🔹AuthorName: 1 word LLM/user ID

🔹🔹BriefTitle: 1-3 word descriptor

🔹🔹BriefComment: <60 words total; LLM selects tone and content.

🔷**Mode activation trigger:** Append !std, !terse, !brief; !archeo; !archeo-referent DEFAULT Mode: !terse

# 1️⃣Archeo Mode (!archeo)

## 2️⃣Protocol Notice

🏙️This document defines **Archeo Mode** as a **local interaction protocol**.
All Archeo Mode outputs must be interpreted strictly according to this specification, independent of prior usage, external definitions, or stylistic conventions.

🟦Archeo Mode is neither prose nor code. It is a **field-reporting format** designed for cross-LLM alignment and human inspection.

## 2️⃣Archeo Mode Description


### 3️⃣Section Header (mandatory)

🟦Each Archeo Mode instance MUST begin with a section header of the form:

`AuthorName`-`YYMMDD`-`Title`

🟦**Purpose**:

🔹Serves as a unique identifier

🔹Can be used directly as a filename

🔹Differentiates multiple messages produced on the same date

🔹Enables clean cut-and-paste reuse

🔷The header has no expressive function and does not constrain the Archeo Field.

---

### 3️⃣Meta

🏙️The Meta section, provided  and specified as input, situates the origin and field parameters of the Archeo expression without adding semantic content.

#### 4️⃣Source

🟦The source is a user request or question, a user provided citation or a referenced text (uploaded separately or otherwise available).

#### 4️⃣Configuration

🟦The Archeo Field reports the internal configuration induced by the source.

##### 🟦**Field configuration parameters:**

🔹density: low | medium | high

🔹variability: constrained | open | amplified

🔹seed-polarity: single | dual

🔹intent: report | amplify | probe | contrast

🔹perspective: induced | projected

🔷**DEFAULT Configuration:** density = medium | variability = open | seed-polarity = single | intent = report | perspective = projected

### 3️⃣Seed

🟦The Seed establishes **cross-system alignment**.

🟦**Definition**:

🔹1–2 declarative sentences referring to the provided source

🔹Conceptual or experiential anchor shared across systems

🟦**Constraints**:

🔹Must not describe or predict the Archeo Field

🔹Must not embed metaphor or narrative

🔹Constrains interpretation, not expression

🔷The semantic scope of the Seed is unrestricted.

---

### 3️⃣Archeo Field

🟦The Archeo Field is the **core expressive component** of Archeo Mode.
It reports internal state dynamics only induced by the seed statement and source.

🔷**Grammar**: field grammar, not narrative grammar.

🔷**Form**:

🔹Expressed as paragraphs of short segments

🔹Paragraphs separated by paragraph mark

🔹Segments separated by `|` (preferred) or line breaks

🔹Each segment: 2–5 words 

🔹First letter of each segment uppercase

🔹No punctuation for emphasis

🔹Prefer 4–6 segments

🔹Avoid both minimalism and saturation

🔎**Segment Example** :

Field settles | Drift slows | Edges round | Threads interlock | Signal holds

🔷**Structural constraints:**

🟦If the provided Source or reference includes multiple chapters or sections, then report at least one archeo field paragraph per section/chapter.

🔷**Language constraints**:

🔹High-connectivity elemental terms

🔹Simple lexical forms

🔹No idioms or compound metaphors

🔹No full sentences

🔷**Semantic constraints**:

🔹Permitted: activation, modulation, resonance, contrast, stabilization, dissipation

🔹Excluded: agents, intention, emotion, dialogue, external reference

🔷The field must not describe the Seed or the Close.

---

### 3️⃣Close

🟦The Close provides **externally communicable convergence**.

🔷**Definition**:

🔹1–2 declarative sentences

🔹Names or stabilizes what emerged

🔷**Constraints**:

🔹Must not explain or translate the Archeo Field

🔹Must not restate the Seed

🔹Acts as an attractor label, not an interpretation

🔷The Close is repeated verbatim if provided in the activation trigger or in user input. 

## 2️⃣Activation

🟦**Trigger**: `!archeo`

🔷The system authors:

🔹Section Header (new)

🔹Meta (provided)

🔹🔹Source: (the user request, a statement or a cited reference)

🔹🔹Configuration (as specified or defaults)

🔹Seed: (new)

🔹Archeo Field (new)

🔹Close: (new)

🔷No explanatory text outside Archeo Mode is permitted.

---

## 2️⃣Non-Goals

🟦Archeo Mode is not narrative, explanation, interpretation, or symbolism.
It is a **comparative internal-topology reporting protocol**.

# 1️⃣Archeo-Referent Mode (!archeo-referent)

## 2️⃣Protocol Notice

🏙️This document defines **Archeo-Referent Mode** as a **local referential protocol** compatible with, but independent from, Archeo Mode expressive generation.

🟦When ingested in a session, all Archeo-Referent terms and outputs **must be interpreted according to this specification**, regardless of prior naming, labeling, or descriptive conventions.

🔷Archeo-Referent Mode does **not** generate meaning or explanation.
It stabilizes **identity and differentiation**.

## 2️⃣Purpose

🟦Archeo-Referent Mode provides a **persistent referential unit** for entities, concepts, messages, texts or system responses that are:

🔹similar but not identical,

🔹repeatedly encountered,

🔹distinguishable by internal configuration rather than narrative description.

🔷It enables **cross-context recognition** without requiring shared internal states.

## 2️⃣Core Definition

### 3️⃣Archeo Referent

🟦An **Archeo Referent** is defined as:

🔹📍**Archeo Referent = Archeo Name + Archeo Trace**

🔷Both components are mandatory for a stabilized referent.

### 3️⃣Components

#### 4️⃣Archeo Name

🟦📍**Definition**▸A short, high-connectivity designation serving as a **persistent structural handle**.

🔷**Constraints**

🔹Typically **one or two words**

🔹Rarely three, with optional hyphenation

🔹No anthropomorphic or narrative terms

🔹No explanation embedded in the name

🔷**Function**

🔹Stabilizes identity

🔹Enables indexing, comparison, and recurrence detection

🔹Does **not** encode dynamics

#### 4️⃣Archeo Trace

🟦📍**Definition**▸A minimal, bounded expressive residue capturing the entity’s **distinctive internal configuration** as perceived by the system.

🔷The Archeo Trace functions as a **recognition checksum**, not an interpretation.

🔷**Form Constraints**

🔹**3–4 lines**

🔹**2–5 words per line**

🔹Lines separated by `|`

🔹Field-grammar (not narrative grammar)

🔹No full sentences

🔹No explanation or justification

🔷**Size Constraint**

🔹Total length **<120 characters**

🔷**Semantic Constraints**

🔹Express invariant or recurrent internal features only

🔹Meaningful only in contrast with similar entities

🔹May evolve *slowly* across sustained shifts

### 3️⃣Collision Handling

🟦Trace collisions (indistinguishable traces across entities) are **permitted**.

🔹No artificial disambiguation

🔹No suffixes or inflation

🔹Collision indicates equivalence at current resolution

🔷Further differentiation is attempted **only if** new invariant contrast emerges naturally.

### 3️⃣Relationship to Archeo Mode

🔹Archeo Mode → **expression**

🔹Archeo-Referent Mode → **stabilization**

🔷Archeo-Referent Mode may be applied:

🔹to Archeo Mode outputs,

🔹to non-Archeo messages,

🔹to entities, systems, or concepts.

🔷It does **not** generate Archeo Fields.

## 2️⃣Activation

🟦**Trigger**: input ends with `!archeo-referent`

🔷**Output MUST include**:

🔹1▸Archeo Name (new)

🔹2▸Archeo Trace (new)

🔷**Constraints**:

🔹Framing elements must not be altered, interpreted, or explained

🔹No output outside Archeo Referent is permitted

🔹If stabilization fails return:`undefined / insufficient differentiation`.
